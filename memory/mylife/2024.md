# 2024

## Land Rover Defender breakdown on the way to Coyote Flats — early 2024

- In early 2024, Dave bought the Land Rover Defender.
- He spent about six months kitting it out for off-roading.
- Upgrades included a winch and a roof rack, getting it ready for trail use.
- Dave and the group headed up the steep hill toward Coyote Flats.
- Near the flatter area at the top of the initial climb, Dave heard a strange sound from the front wheels.
- He kept going, but the air suspension had somehow failed, leaving the Defender with no suspension.
- Dave thinks he hit a rock and damaged a tire.
- The group had to stop and fix the tire.
- After that, Dave realized the Defender was not going to make it much farther.
- Dave stayed in one spot while everybody else carried on.
- The Defender was in bad shape: flat tire, damaged tire, no air suspension, and the engine was having real trouble starting.
- Dave spent one night alone.
- In the morning, people came back to help.
- They patched the tires and decided Dave would have to creep back down the hill with no suspension.
- The crew got Dave to another camp closer to the top of the descending trail back to town.
- Dave spent another night there wondering whether he would be able to make it out, whether the Defender would start, and whether he could get down the hill.
